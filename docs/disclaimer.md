# Medical and Editorial Disclaimer

This educational reference is intended for medical students, residents, fellows, nurses, cath-lab technologists, and other healthcare professionals. It does not provide patient-specific medical advice or replace formal training.

Coronary intervention should be performed only by appropriately trained teams in properly equipped facilities. Device selection, medication use, procedural technique, and complication management must follow current guidelines, local protocols, and the applicable manufacturer's instructions for use.

News items may summarize early or incomplete evidence. Readers should consult the original publication, regulatory notice, guideline, or safety communication before applying information clinically.

The site maintains a draft-first editorial process. A draft is not an endorsement, recommendation, or statement of standard of care.
