# Diagnostic and Guide Catheters

## Diagnostic catheters

Diagnostic catheters deliver contrast into the coronary arteries and allow angiographic assessment of anatomy, flow, lesions, grafts, and complications. Common designs include Judkins, Amplatz, multipurpose, internal mammary, bypass-graft, and pigtail catheters.

## Guide catheters

Guide catheters provide a working channel for guidewires, balloons, stents, imaging catheters, and other devices. Support depends on curve selection, coaxial alignment, aortic-root anatomy, catheter stiffness, and access route.

## Important precautions

Observe the pressure waveform during engagement. Damping or ventricularization may indicate ostial compromise or deep engagement. Avoid forceful manipulation and unnecessary contrast injection when pressure is abnormal.
