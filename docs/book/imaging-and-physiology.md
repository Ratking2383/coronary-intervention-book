# Intravascular Imaging and Physiological Assessment

## IVUS

Intravascular ultrasound provides cross-sectional information about vessel size, plaque burden, calcium, lesion length, stent expansion, apposition, and edge pathology.

## OCT

Optical coherence tomography provides high-resolution visualization of stent struts, thrombus, tissue prolapse, edge dissection, malapposition, and neoatherosclerosis. It usually requires blood clearance and therefore may be less suitable in severe kidney dysfunction or unstable hemodynamics.

## Pressure-based physiology

FFR and resting pressure indices help determine whether an angiographically intermediate lesion is physiologically significant. Calibration, equalization, drift assessment, and correct wire position are essential.
