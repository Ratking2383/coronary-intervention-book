# Complications and Safety

Potential complications include coronary dissection, perforation, acute vessel closure, stent thrombosis, no-reflow, arrhythmia, bleeding, contrast-associated kidney injury, and access-site complications.

## General principles

- Recognize deterioration early.
- Maintain communication with the entire cath-lab team.
- Preserve wire position when safe.
- Reassess flow, pressure, anatomy, and hemodynamics.
- Activate surgical or mechanical-support backup when indicated.
- Follow institutional emergency protocols.

This chapter is an educational overview, not a procedural emergency manual.
