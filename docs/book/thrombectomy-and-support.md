# Thrombectomy, Embolic Protection, and Hemodynamic Support

## Thrombus aspiration

Aspiration may be used selectively in high-thrombus-burden situations. Risks include embolization, stroke, dissection, perforation, and vessel injury; routine use is not appropriate for every intervention.

## Distal embolic protection

Filter or occlusion systems may capture debris during selected interventions, particularly some saphenous vein-graft procedures.

## Mechanical circulatory support

Intra-aortic balloon pumps, percutaneous ventricular support pumps, and extracorporeal support may be considered in carefully selected patients with shock or high-risk intervention. Risks include bleeding, vascular injury, limb ischemia, hemolysis, thrombosis, and infection.
