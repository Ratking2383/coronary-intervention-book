# Balloon Catheters and Coronary Stents

## Balloon catheters

Balloons are used for lesion crossing, predilation, post-dilation, focal treatment, and stent optimization.

### Semicompliant balloons

These expand progressively with pressure and are often used for predilation and less resistant lesions.

### Noncompliant balloons

These provide more predictable expansion at higher pressure and are commonly used for post-dilation and correction of stent underexpansion.

### Specialty balloons

Cutting, scoring, and drug-coated balloons may be selected for fibrotic disease, selected restenosis, or situations where avoiding another permanent implant is desirable.

## Coronary stents

Drug-eluting stents consist of a metallic scaffold and an antiproliferative drug-delivery system. Important performance characteristics include radial strength, flexibility, deliverability, expansion range, radiopacity, and strut design.

## Optimization goals

A deployed stent should adequately cover the diseased segment, be expanded to an appropriate diameter, and be apposed to the vessel wall without compromising important side branches.
