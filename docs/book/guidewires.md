# Coronary Guidewires

## Function

A guidewire crosses the target lesion and provides a rail for balloons, stents, microcatheters, and imaging devices.

## Key characteristics

- Tip load
- Torque response
- Support
- Trackability
- Coating and lubricity
- Radiopacity
- Distal flexibility

## Common categories

### Workhorse wires

General-purpose wires balance safety, flexibility, torque, and support.

### Support wires

These improve device delivery in tortuous, calcified, or long lesions but may increase vessel straightening and injury risk.

### Polymer-jacketed and specialty wires

These may help navigate tortuous anatomy or chronic total occlusions. They require careful fluoroscopic control because tactile feedback may be reduced.

## Handling principles

Shape the distal tip appropriately, advance without force, maintain fluoroscopic awareness, and confirm distal position before delivering equipment.
