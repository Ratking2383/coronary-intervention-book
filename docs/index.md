# Coronary Intervention Living Book

Welcome to an evolving educational reference on the materials, devices, techniques, evidence, and safety considerations used in coronary intervention.

!!! warning "Educational use"
    This site is for education and reference. It is not a substitute for supervised interventional cardiology training, institutional protocols, manufacturer instructions for use, or current professional guidelines.

## What this site covers

- Vascular access and catheter-laboratory equipment
- Guide catheters and coronary guidewires
- Balloon catheters and coronary stents
- Calcium modification and lesion preparation
- Intravascular imaging and physiological assessment
- Thrombectomy, embolic protection, and circulatory support
- Procedural complications and safety
- Time-stamped daily news and evidence updates

## How updates work

News and chapter revisions are prepared as drafts, reviewed for source quality and clinical context, and published only after editorial approval. See the [Daily News](news/index.md) section for the update archive.
