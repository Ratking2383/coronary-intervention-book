# Coronary Intervention Living Book Launch

!!! warning "Draft — review required"
    This is the initial project draft. It has not been medically reviewed or approved for publication.

## Purpose

The Coronary Intervention Living Book is being developed as an evolving educational reference on coronary-intervention materials, devices, tools, techniques, evidence, and safety.

## Planned update process

Daily research will identify potentially relevant publications, guidelines, regulatory announcements, device updates, and safety notices. Each item will be checked against its original source and prepared as a draft before any public release.

## Editorial safeguards

News items will distinguish peer-reviewed evidence, professional guidance, regulatory information, manufacturer communications, and expert commentary. Early or incomplete evidence will be labeled clearly.
